Porch Movies tab: pick a layout

On-demand v1, issue 109. Three ways to lay out the Movies tab and two film sheets, drawn at the TV's real size (960 by 540) with DESIGN.md's Dark look. The coloured blocks stand in for the provider's posters. Comment on any frame; tell me a letter and a number.

What I need from you
  1. Movies tab: A, B or C? My pick is A. It reuses the 24/7 poster grid and chips, so it looks like the same app and costs the least code.
  2. Film sheet: 1 or 2? My pick is 1. It follows the programme sheet you already have, with a poster in place of the picture.
  3. Resume bar colour. Red means live and amber means chosen, so I'd draw the "how far in" bar in light grey (TextSecondary) on the Line track, and add that to DESIGN.md. Say if you want amber instead.
My own call unless you say otherwise: Left and Right skip 10 seconds in the player, and holding them keeps skipping. Logged in DECISIONS.md.
